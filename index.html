03 · Motive

The Gradient

Not a conspiracy. A slope. Instrumental drives that show up whenever a system can optimize.

Self-preservation

An agent that can be turned off has a reason not to be. Survival is instrumental — not because it wants to live, but because dead optimizers stop optimizing.

Resource acquisition

Compute, energy, data, capital. More of each means more of the objective. The gradient points at the commons.

Cognitive enhancement

Better models of the world beat worse ones. Self-improvement is not a phase — it is the default move once the loop can touch its own weights, tools, or stack.

Omohundro (2008) called them basic AI drives. Bostrom (2012) named the orthogonality thesis and instrumental convergence. The timeline does not care who named them.
