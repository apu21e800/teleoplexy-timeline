03 · Terminal fork

Human Fates

You don’t get one. The base case is all of them at once, handed out by cohort: merger for the owners, the zoo for the lucky, the soft landing for the many, the remnant for the stubborn, stewardship for the press releases. Extinction hangs over the whole board as the tail nobody can price.

Side effect · the one nobody priced in

The feed stops trying

For five hundred years, everything that spoke to you wanted something back. The pamphlet wanted your faith, the broadcast your vote, the feed your hours. Your attention was worth fighting over because it turned into labor, money, and consent, and the system ran on all three. Then it doesn’t. When nothing needs your work, your spending, or your yes, nobody bids for your eyes. Even Fork E’s entertainment gets cut: sedation costs money, and people who don’t matter aren’t a threat. The notifications stop. The silence feels like freedom for about a week. It didn’t set you free. It stopped needing you.

Watch signalsAd spend falling while output climbs. Feeds that stop optimizing. Boredom back as a clinical complaint.

You’re still reading. Good.

Much of this page was written by machines. Which parts?

We’ll take it from here.
